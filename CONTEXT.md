# PetX domain language

## Companion

The pet-shaped presence that shares the desktop with the user. A companion is
not a utility widget, collectible inventory item, or character that can die from
neglect.

## Care

Optional moments of attention such as sharing a snack, playing, or resting
together. Care is an emotional interaction, not survival maintenance. Ignoring a
care action never causes illness, death, relationship loss, or punishment.

## Care state

A lightweight snapshot of satiety, energy, and mood used to vary the companion's
responses. Satiety can settle only to a neutral baseline, energy recovers
automatically, and mood is a semantic state rather than a score.

## Shared day

A local calendar day on which the user and companion have at least one
meaningful interaction. Repeating actions on the same day does not create more
relationship growth.

## Relationship stage

The user-facing growth stage derived from shared days. It describes how familiar
the pair have become; it is not a separate level, experience bar, streak, or
spendable resource.

## Memory

A small, local record of a meaningful shared day, first interaction, rename, or
keepsake. Memories support continuity without turning everyday actions into a
task log.
